Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Practical questions from home and business owners across Ernakulam.

How much does CCTV installation cost?

Pricing depends on the number of cameras, cabling distance and DVR/NVR storage capacity. Contact us for an on-site inspection or a phone estimate.

What type of CCTV camera is suitable for my home?

Dome cameras suit indoor ceilings, sit-outs and covered porches. Bullet cameras suit outdoor gates and compound walls. We can help you choose based on your property.

Do you install Hikvision and CP Plus cameras?

Yes, we regularly install and configure both Hikvision and CP Plus systems, along with selected Wi-Fi cameras suited to your requirement.

Can I view my CCTV cameras from my phone?

Where your equipment and internet connection support it, we help set up the relevant mobile app on your Android or iPhone device for remote viewing.

Do you install standalone Wi-Fi cameras?

Yes. For smaller homes, flats, or monitoring a specific zone, standalone Wi-Fi cameras provide a cable-minimal option.

Can you upgrade my existing CCTV system?

It depends on your existing wiring and equipment. Contact us with details of your current setup and we can advise whether an upgrade or expansion is possible.

Do you provide CCTV repair and maintenance?

Get in touch with details of the issue you're facing — we can advise whether on-site troubleshooting support is available for your specific system.

Which areas do you provide service in?

We primarily serve Ernakulam city, Kochi, Aluva, Kalamassery, North Paravur, Angamaly, Tripunithura and nearby areas.
