Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Practical queries from home and business owners across Ernakulam.

How much does CCTV installation cost? expand_more

Pricing depends on the total number of cameras, cabling distance required, whether conduit piping is used, and the DVR/NVR hard drive capacity. Contact us for a straightforward on-site inspection or a quick upfront phone estimate without hidden charges.

What type of CCTV camera is suitable for my home? expand_more

Dome cameras are ideal for indoor ceilings, sit-outs, and covered porches due to their unobtrusive shape. Weatherproof bullet cameras provide longer range, focused infrared coverage, and outdoor deterrence for gates and compound walls.

Do you install Hikvision and CP Plus cameras? expand_more

Yes, we regularly install and configure both Hikvision and CP Plus systems, as well as selected standalone smart Wi-Fi cameras. We select genuine models known for reliability in Kerala's coastal humid climate.

Can I view my CCTV cameras from my phone? expand_more

Yes. Provided your property has an active internet connection (broadband/Wi-Fi router), we configure the official mobile applications (Hik-Connect, gCMOB, etc.) directly on your family or staff members' Android or iPhone devices.

Do you install standalone Wi-Fi cameras? expand_more

Yes. For smaller homes, flats, or monitoring single specific zones (like an elderly parent's room or front doorway), standalone Wi-Fi cameras with microSD card storage provide a quick, cable-minimal security solution.

Can you upgrade my existing CCTV system? expand_more

Yes, we can test your existing coaxial or Cat6 wiring and replace outdated analog cameras with modern high-definition units, or expand storage capacity by installing higher-capacity surveillance hard drives.

Do you provide CCTV repair and maintenance? expand_more

Yes. We diagnose video signal loss, replace failing 12V power supply units, address continuous DVR beeping, fix recording disk errors, and realign cameras shifted by monsoon winds or painting work.

Which areas do you provide service in? expand_more

We primarily serve Ernakulam city, Kochi, Aluva, Kalamassery, Kakkanad, North Paravur, Angamaly, and surrounding residential suburbs across Ernakulam district.
