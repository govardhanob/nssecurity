## Brand & Style

The design system projects unyielding reliability, technical precision, and community accountability for residential, commercial, and industrial clients across Ernakulam and greater Kochi. The design aesthetic is **Corporate / Modern** anchored in functional minimalism: restrained, authoritative, and clean without corporate detachment.

### Emotional Tone
- **Vigilant & Steadfast:** Communicates 24/7 technical competence and enduring physical protection.
- **Approachable Authority:** Professional local expertise that respects residential homeowners and enterprise facility managers alike.
- **Uncluttered Transparency:** Clear pricing tiers, unambiguous service breakdowns, and straightforward quotation paths.

### Design Principles
1. **Clarity Over Ornament:** Avoid decorative clutter, camera graphics, or fake futuristic tech overlays. Rely on pristine typography, structured grids, and authentic on-site photography.
2. **Restrained Urgency:** Reserve high-chroma safety amber strictly for critical actions (quotation requests, emergency service triggers, key badges).
3. **Engineered Precision:** Crisp 1px structural borders, hairline dividers, and disciplined whitespace reflect meticulous wiring, mounting, and system architecture.

## Colors

The palette leverages a deep naval foundation, reflecting defense and structural stability, paired with a disciplined safety amber/orange utilized solely for high-conversion intents.

### Functional Roles
- **Primary Navy (`#0f172a`):** Primary headings, header navigation background, core icon accents, and primary text layers.
- **Secondary Amber/Orange (`#ea580c`):** High-priority CTAs ("Book Site Survey", "Request Quote"), active radio states, and priority badges. Never used for full-surface card backgrounds or lengthy text.
- **Tertiary Slate (`#1e293b`):** Sub-surfaces, secondary button borders, technical spec callout boxes, and table headers.
- **Neutral Charcoal (`#64748b`):** Secondary text, caption labels, border outlines, and metadata strings.
- **Surface Canvas (`#ffffff`):** Base canvas and interactive card foregrounds.
- **Surface Muted (`#f8fafc`):** Alternating section strips, filter backgrounds, and input field fills.
- **Border Subdued (`#e2e8f0`):** Hairline container divides and table borders ensuring AA compliance against text.

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans governs the entire hierarchy. Its balanced humanist curves and contemporary geometric structure allow high legibility across hardware specification tables, price estimates, and high-impact hero headings.

- **Headings (`display-lg` through `headline-sm`):** Rendered in deep navy (`#0f172a`) with subtle negative tracking for an engineered, trustworthy stance.
- **Body Copies (`body-lg` through `body-sm`):** Maintained in slate tones (`#334155`) rather than harsh absolute black, improving reading comfort during long service evaluations.
- **Labels & Numbers (`label-md`, `label-sm`):** Optimized for phone numbers, SLA badges, ISO accreditations, and surveillance camera spec readouts (e.g., 4K, ColorVu, IP67).

## Layout & Spacing

A 12-column responsive fluid grid provides disciplined modularity across screen sizes.

### Breakpoints & Layout Adaptations
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `margin-mobile` (16px) and `gutter-mobile` (16px). Full-width button actions and stacked contact quick-actions (WhatsApp / Phone call floating tray).
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with 24px margins. Two-column service packaging grids.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout capped at a maximum container width of `1200px` to maintain optimal line lengths and focus.

### Rhythmic Discipline
- Internal card padding strictly adopts `space-lg` (24px) for desktop and `space-md` (16px) on mobile screens.
- Form inputs, spec grids, and feature checklists consistently rely on `space-sm` (8px) and `space-md` (16px) element gaps.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects heavy, muddy shadows in favor of a low-elevation, high-clarity surface strategy matching a disciplined field security firm.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat Base):** Background canvas (`#ffffff`) and tinted section bands (`#f8fafc`). Completely flat with no shadow.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Standard Containers):** Crisp 1px solid border (`#e2e8f0`) backed by an ambient, low-contrast blur:
  `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05);`
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns, Floating Call Buttons, Hovered Cards):**
  `box-shadow: 0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03);`
- **Level 3 (Modals & Quote Overlays):**
  `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04);`

Hover interactions slightly translate cards upward (-2px) while transitioning the border color from `#e2e8f0` to `#cbd5e1`.

## Shapes

The roundedness level is **Soft (1)** (4px base, 8px `rounded-lg`). 

- **Buttons, Inputs & Badges:** Use 6px - 8px radius (`rounded-lg`). This softens the overall utilitarian tone while maintaining an architectural, sturdy feel suitable for a technical security provider.
- **Cards & Modal Windows:** Fixed at 8px (`rounded-lg`) corner radii to maintain clean perimeter alignment with technical icons and photography.
- **Pills/Circles:** Strictly reserved for status dots (e.g., green "Available for 24/7 Support" indicator) and floating contact bubble triggers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Conversion):** Safety amber background (`#ea580c`), white text, semi-bold (`label-md`), 8px radius. Active state darkens to `#c2410c`. Used for: "Request Free Site Survey", "Get Instant Quote".
- **Secondary Action (Inquiry):** Deep navy background (`#0f172a`), white text. Used for: "Call Technicians", "View CCTV Packages".
- **Outline / Ghost Action:** White background, 1px border (`#cbd5e1`), navy text (`#0f172a`). Hover shifts to `#f8fafc`.

### Input Fields & Selects
- 1px border (`#cbd5e1`) on background `#ffffff`. Focused state switches to a 1.5px navy ring (`#0f172a`) with a 3px soft outer ring (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`).
- Error states swap border to `#dc2626` with clean contextual helper text beneath.

### Service & Pricing Cards
- Clean white background framed with 1px hairline border (`#e2e8f0`).
- Feature lists utilize deep navy check icons paired with `body-sm` typography.
- "Popular in Ernakulam" or "Best for Homes" featured cards add a 1.5px amber border (`#ea580c`) and a crisp top-right ribbon badge.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square with 4px radius (checkbox) and 100% radius (radio).
- 1.5px border (`#94a3b8`). Checked state fills with secondary amber (`#ea580c`) featuring a crisp white tick.

### Badges & Verification Tags
- Subtle, low-chroma fills: light slate background (`#f1f5f9`), slate navy text (`#334155`), 4px border radius.
- Safety / Guarantee Badges: Light amber background (`#fff7ed`), dark amber text (`#9a3412`), subtle border (`#ffedd5`). Used for: "Hikvision Certified", "CP PLUS Partner", "Kerala Police Verification Compliant".

### Local Trust Header Strip
- Top persistent emergency bar in `#0f172a` displaying direct telephone access, WhatsApp chat trigger, and primary operational coverage: "Serving Ernakulam, Aluva, Kakkanad, Fort Kochi & Tripunithura".
